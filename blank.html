Common probability distributions — pick a family, drag its parameters, watch the density and moments update live. Continuous families draw a smooth PDF; discrete families draw a PMF as bars.

Make a market and survive the flow. A hidden fair value is the sum of the dice — you know its EV, not the roll. Quote a bid and an ask: informed traders know the value and pick you off, while noise traders trade either way but walk if your quote is too wide. Too tight and the informed bleed you; too wide and nobody trades.

Edge math: against noise flow your expected profit per fill is your distance from EV; against informed flow you lose. Widening cuts adverse selection but also cuts noise volume — the sweet spot is a moderate market centred on EV.

Linear regression by simulation — set the TRUE intercept, slope, noise level, and sample size, then watch ordinary least squares try to recover them. The fit chases the truth; crank the noise and the estimates wobble.
